    1. Add App to Your Phone

    1. 1

      iPhone (Safari)

      Open your hosted URL in Safari, tap the Share icon, then choose Add to Home Screen.

    2. 2

      Android (Chrome)

      Open the URL in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

    2. Free Phone Push & News Engine

    1. 1

      Install the free ntfy app

      Download ntfy from the Apple App Store or Google Play, tap +, and subscribe to your private topic from the Alerts tab.

    2. 2

      Enable GitHub Actions Secrets & Variables

      In your GitHub repo → Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions, add NTFY_TOPIC (and optionally GEMINI_API_KEY for AI-written daily briefings):

      NTFY_TOPIC=your-private-topic GOLD_THRESHOLD_PCT=1.5 SILVER_THRESHOLD_PCT=3.0 CATALYST_ALERTS=true
